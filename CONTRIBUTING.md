# Contributing

The playbook grows by harvesting: after a plugin is done, the lessons from its session come back here (`docs/harvesting.md` has the prompt and where each kind of lesson goes). Pull requests and issues are both welcome.

## What to send

- **Rules and the incident behind them**, in your own words, with the number that proves it and how it's verified (on hardware, in tests, or only believed).
- **Generic tools** that help every plugin, with a docstring.
- **Corrections** to anything here that turned out wrong.

You don't need to open your plugin to contribute. A lesson from a closed-source or commercial plugin is welcome: share the rule and what happened, not the code.

## What not to send

- **Code you don't own.** Snippets from other people's projects can't come in unless their licence is MIT-compatible and you say where they're from. GPL code can't come in at all (the playbook is MIT). Describe the technique in your own words instead.
- **Plugin-specific sound design**, preset names or tuning: they don't help a different plugin.
- **Third-party art or fonts**, unless their licence allows it and is noted.

## Licence and credit

Contributions are under the MIT licence, like the rest of the playbook (`LICENSE`); by sending a pull request you agree to that. Each harvest is credited in `CHANGELOG.md`: what it came from and who contributed it.
